# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our PocketSafar community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

### Respectful Communication Rules

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our community include:

* **Demonstrating empathy and kindness** toward other people
* **Being respectful** of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
* **Using welcoming and inclusive language** in all communications
* **Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback**
* **Accepting responsibility** and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from the experience
* **Focusing on what is best** not just for us as individuals, but for the overall community
* **Supporting collaboration** between backend (FastAPI, ML, data processing) and frontend (React, UI/UX) teams
* **Maintaining high-quality standards** in code reviews for both API design and user interface implementations
* **Documenting your work** thoroughly to help other contributors understand backend algorithms and frontend components
* **Being patient** with newcomers and those learning
* **Respecting different skill levels** and areas of expertise

### No Discrimination or Harassment

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

* The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
* Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
* Public or private harassment of any form
* Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission
* Dismissing or belittling contributions based on whether they are backend or frontend focused
* Discriminatory jokes, comments, or language
* Unwelcome comments regarding a person's lifestyle choices and practices
* Deliberate intimidation, stalking, or following
* Sustained disruption of discussions or collaborative work
* Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces, including:

* GitHub repository (issues, pull requests, discussions)
* Code reviews for backend (Python, FastAPI, ML pipelines) and frontend (React, TypeScript, UI components)
* Community communications and collaboration channels
* Representing the project in public spaces
* Any interaction related to the PocketSafar project

This Code of Conduct also applies when an individual is officially representing the community in public spaces.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Community leaders have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Reporting and Escalation Procedures

### How to Report Issues

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, or have any other concerns, please report it by:

1. **Creating a confidential issue**: Open a GitHub issue with the `code-of-conduct` label
2. **Email**: Send a detailed report to conduct@pocketsafar.org
3. **Direct contact**: For urgent or sensitive matters, contact the project maintainers directly

### What to Include in Your Report

When reporting an issue, please include:

* Your contact information (for follow-up)
* Names (real, usernames, or pseudonyms) of any individuals involved
* Description of the incident, including:
  - What happened
  - When it happened
  - Where it happened (which repository, channel, etc.)
* Any supporting documentation (screenshots, links, etc.)
* Whether you prefer confidentiality
* Any other information you believe would be helpful

### Response Process

1. **Acknowledgment**: We will acknowledge receipt of your report within 48 hours
2. **Review**: The community leadership team will review the incident
3. **Investigation**: We may request additional information or clarification
4. **Decision**: We will determine appropriate action based on our guidelines
5. **Communication**: We will communicate our decision to all parties involved
6. **Follow-up**: We will follow up to ensure the issue has been resolved

### Escalation Path

If you are not satisfied with the initial response:

1. Request a review from additional community leaders
2. Escalate to the project steering committee
3. For serious violations, we may involve GitHub's community support

## Direct Contact for Issues

For immediate assistance or confidential reporting:

* **Email**: conduct@pocketsafar.org
* **Project Maintainers**: You can also reach out directly to project maintainers via GitHub
* **GitHub Issues**: Create a confidential issue with the `code-of-conduct` label

All reports will be handled with discretion and confidentiality.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Community leaders will follow these Community Impact Guidelines in determining the consequences for any action they deem in violation of this Code of Conduct:

### 1. Correction

**Community Impact**: Use of inappropriate language or other behavior deemed unprofessional or unwelcome in the community.

**Consequence**: A private, written warning from community leaders, providing clarity around the nature of the violation and an explanation of why the behavior was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.

### 2. Warning

**Community Impact**: A violation through a single incident or series of actions.

**Consequence**: A warning with consequences for continued behavior. No interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited interaction with those enforcing the Code of Conduct, for a specified period of time. This includes avoiding interactions in community spaces as well as external channels like social media. Violating these terms may lead to a temporary or permanent ban.

### 3. Temporary Ban

**Community Impact**: A serious violation of community standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior.

**Consequence**: A temporary ban from any sort of interaction or public communication with the community for a specified period of time. No public or private interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited interaction with those enforcing the Code of Conduct, is allowed during this period. Violating these terms may lead to a permanent ban.

### 4. Permanent Ban

**Community Impact**: Demonstrating a pattern of violation of community standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior, harassment of an individual, or aggression toward or disparagement of classes of individuals.

**Consequence**: A permanent ban from any sort of public interaction within the community.
